MODULE 018 MINUTES

Phishing

Pause before you click.

Phishing is the most common way attackers target financial organisations. Their messages are made to look ordinary, but a deliberate pause helps us protect Blossom and our members.

⌁

Phishing

A broad, deceptive message designed to steal information or access.

At Blossom, one click can expose trusted systems.
◎

Spear phishing

A targeted message tailored to a person or team.

Attackers may use public details to make it convincing.
♛

Whaling

A targeted attack impersonating or targeting senior leaders.

It can create pressure around sensitive decisions.
↗

BEC

Business email compromise manipulates payment or data requests.

Always verify financial requests out of band.

MODULE 027 MINUTES

Compromised credentials

Your account is a
valuable asset.

Employee accounts can provide a path into member information and internal tools. Strong passwords, a password manager and MFA all work together to prevent account takeover.

TRY IT

Build a stronger password

This check happens only in your browser. Nothing you type is stored.

Use 12+ characters with a mix of letters, numbers and symbols.

MFA SCENARIO

Your phone shows a sign-in prompt you didn’t start.

What would you do?

YOU
Shopping siteSocial accountWork account

Reusing a password turns one breached website into a possible route to your Blossom account.

Remember

Use a password manager to create unique passwords, protect MFA codes, and report unexpected prompts.

MODULE 038 MINUTES

Insider threat

Security is in the
everyday.

Insider threats are often accidental, not malicious. Secure handling, least privilege and speaking up early protect members in the normal flow of work.

WORKPLACE SCENARIOScenario 1 of 5

Customer spreadsheet request

You receive a spreadsheet containing customer financial information. A colleague from another team asks you to email it to them.

WOULD YOU REPORT IT?

Small signals matter.

Choose report or ignore for each situation.

01

Lock your screen whenever you step away.

02

Share documents only through approved tools.

03

Use only the access you need to do your role.

04

Keep member information out of public conversations.

05

Report mistakes and concerns as soon as you notice them.
